Overall milestones: the landmark papers that defined AI as a whole, from the perceptron to modern reasoning models. For deeper per-field history — including recent LLM and AI-agent work — see the Area Timelines tab.

YearPaperAreaWhy it matters
1950 Computing Machinery and IntelligenceAlan Turing Foundations Posed "Can machines think?" and proposed the imitation game (Turing test), framing the field before it had a name.
1958 The PerceptronFrank Rosenblatt Neural Nets The first trainable artificial neuron — the ancestor of every neural network that followed.
1986 Learning Representations by Back-Propagating ErrorsRumelhart, Hinton & Williams Neural Nets Popularized backpropagation, making multi-layer networks trainable and reviving neural-network research.
1997 Long Short-Term MemoryHochreiter & Schmidhuber Sequences Solved vanishing gradients for recurrent networks; dominated speech and language modeling for two decades.
1998 Gradient-Based Learning Applied to Document Recognition (LeNet-5)LeCun et al. CV Established convolutional neural networks as the way to read images, decades ahead of the hardware.
2012 ImageNet Classification with Deep CNNs (AlexNet)Krizhevsky, Sutskever & Hinton CV Crushed the ImageNet benchmark with GPUs and ignited the modern deep-learning era.
2013 Efficient Estimation of Word Representations (word2vec)Mikolov et al. NLP Showed word meaning could be captured in vectors ("king − man + woman ≈ queen"), starting the embeddings era.
2013 Playing Atari with Deep Reinforcement Learning (DQN)Mnih et al., DeepMind RL First deep network to learn control from raw pixels, launching deep reinforcement learning.
2014 Generative Adversarial NetworksGoodfellow et al. Generative Two networks competing — generator vs. discriminator — made realistic image generation possible.
2014 Sequence to Sequence Learning with Neural NetworksSutskever, Vinyals & Le NLP The encoder–decoder recipe behind neural machine translation and, later, chat models.
2014 Adam: A Method for Stochastic OptimizationKingma & Ba Optimization Became the default optimizer for nearly every deep model trained since.
2015 Deep Residual Learning (ResNet)He et al. CV Skip connections let networks go from 20 layers to 1000+, a trick every modern architecture inherits.
2016 Mastering the Game of Go (AlphaGo)Silver et al., DeepMind RL Beat the world's best Go players a decade ahead of predictions; deep RL's defining public moment.
2017 Attention Is All You Need (Transformer)Vaswani et al. Architecture The Transformer replaced recurrence with attention — the architecture underneath every modern LLM.
2018 BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional TransformersDevlin et al., Google NLP Made "pretrain, then fine-tune" the standard playbook for language understanding.
2020 Scaling Laws for Neural Language ModelsKaplan et al., OpenAI Scaling Showed loss falls predictably with compute, data, and parameters — the argument for building ever-bigger models.
2020 Language Models are Few-Shot Learners (GPT-3)Brown et al., OpenAI LLM 175B parameters revealed in-context learning: one model, many tasks, no fine-tuning.
2020 Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic ModelsHo, Jain & Abbeel Generative Made diffusion practical, displacing GANs as the engine of image generation.
2020 An Image is Worth 16x16 Words (ViT)Dosovitskiy et al., Google CV Brought the Transformer to vision, ending CNNs' monopoly on image models.
2021 Learning Transferable Visual Models (CLIP)Radford et al., OpenAI Multimodal Tied images and text into one embedding space — the connective tissue of multimodal AI.
2021 Highly Accurate Protein Structure Prediction (AlphaFold 2)Jumper et al., DeepMind AI for Science Effectively solved protein folding, a 50-year grand challenge; later earned a Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
2022 Training LMs to Follow Instructions (InstructGPT)Ouyang et al., OpenAI Alignment RLHF turned raw language models into helpful assistants — the technique behind ChatGPT.
2022 Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits ReasoningWei et al., Google Reasoning "Think step by step" unlocked latent reasoning in LLMs and seeded today's reasoning models.
2022 Training Compute-Optimal LLMs (Chinchilla)Hoffmann et al., DeepMind Scaling Showed most LLMs were undertrained on data, resetting how the field balances model size vs. tokens.
2022 High-Resolution Image Synthesis with Latent DiffusionRombach et al. Generative Ran diffusion in latent space, making it cheap — the paper behind Stable Diffusion.
2023 LLaMA: Open and Efficient Foundation Language ModelsTouvron et al., Meta Open Models Put near-frontier weights in researchers' hands and ignited the open-model ecosystem.
2023 Mamba: Linear-Time Sequence ModelingGu & Dao Architecture State-space models as the first credible post-Transformer challenger for long sequences.
2025 DeepSeek-R1: Incentivizing Reasoning via RLDeepSeek Reasoning Showed reasoning ability can emerge from pure reinforcement learning — and released it openly at a fraction of frontier cost.

Large Language Models

From the Transformer to reasoning models: the fastest-moving decade in the field.

  • 2017

    Transformer — Attention Is All You Need

    Attention replaces recurrence; the architecture every LLM still uses.

  • 2018

    GPT-1 & BERT — the pretraining era begins

    Generative pretraining (OpenAI) and bidirectional pretraining (Google) make "pretrain, then adapt" the standard recipe.

  • 2019

    GPT-2 — scale brings fluency

    1.5B parameters write coherent long-form text; its staged release starts the first big debate on releasing model weights.

  • 2020

    GPT-3 & the scaling laws

    In-context learning emerges at 175B parameters, while Kaplan et al. show performance scales predictably with compute — the case for going bigger.

  • 2022

    Chinchilla, InstructGPT, and ChatGPT

    Compute-optimal training resets scaling strategy; RLHF makes models helpful; ChatGPT (Nov 30) takes LLMs from research to a hundred million users.

  • 2023

    GPT-4 and the open-weights wave

    GPT-4 passes professional exams and accepts images; LLaMA and Llama 2 put strong weights in the open; Mistral 7B shows small can be mighty.

  • 2024

    Multimodal by default, and test-time compute

    GPT-4o and Gemini 1.5 (1M-token context) make voice and vision native; OpenAI's o1 shows models improve by thinking longer, not just training bigger.

  • 2025

    DeepSeek-V3 / R1 — the open frontier

    Near-frontier capability at a fraction of the training cost, with reasoning learned by pure RL — reasoning models become the industry default.

  • 2026

    Frontier open models target software engineering

    Releases like GLM-5 push open models toward real-world coding and long-horizon agentic work at falling cost.

AI Agents

LLMs that act: tools, computers, codebases — the area moving fastest right now.

  • 2022

    ReAct — reason, then act

    Interleaving chain-of-thought with tool actions becomes the basic control loop of nearly every agent since.

  • 2023

    Toolformer & the autonomous-loop moment

    Models teach themselves to call APIs; AutoGPT and BabyAGI go viral as the first self-directed task loops the public can run.

  • 2023

    Generative Agents & Voyager

    Simulated agent societies with memory and planning (the "Smallville" paper); Voyager learns an ever-growing skill library in Minecraft.

  • 2023

    SWE-bench — agents meet real work

    Resolving actual GitHub issues becomes the benchmark that defines coding-agent progress.

  • 2024

    Coding agents arrive: Devin & SWE-agent

    End-to-end software agents plan, edit, run, and debug; agent–computer interface design proves as important as the model.

  • 2024

    Computer use & the Model Context Protocol

    Anthropic's computer-use API lets models operate a real desktop; MCP standardizes how agents connect to tools and data.

  • 2025

    Agents in production

    Deep-research agents, terminal coding agents (Claude Code, Codex), and multi-agent orchestration move from demos to daily workflows.

  • 2026

    Self-evolving skills

    Work like SkillOpt has agents optimize their own skill libraries in text space, with no added inference cost — agents that improve themselves on the job.

Computer Vision

From reading digits to promptable, open-vocabulary perception.

  • 1998

    LeNet-5 — CNNs read checks

    Convolution + pooling + gradient descent, deployed commercially decades before the deep-learning boom.

  • 2012

    AlexNet — the ImageNet moment

    GPU-trained CNN halves the error rate of classical pipelines and converts the field to deep learning overnight.

  • 2015

    ResNet, and detection gets fast

    Residual connections unlock very deep networks; Faster R-CNN and YOLO make real-time object detection practical.

  • 2017

    Mask R-CNN — pixel-level understanding

    One framework for detection, segmentation, and pose — the workhorse of applied vision for years.

  • 2020

    ViT — Transformers take over vision

    Image patches as tokens; with enough data, attention beats convolution's built-in assumptions.

  • 2021

    CLIP — vision meets language

    400M image–text pairs give open-vocabulary recognition: classify anything you can describe.

  • 2023

    Segment Anything (SAM)

    A promptable foundation model for segmentation; SAM 2 (2024) extends it to video.

  • 2026

    Dense spatial perception & streaming 3D

    Current work pushes sub-pixel boundary understanding and real-time 3D reconstruction — vision as continuous geometry, not just labels.

Generative Media

Images, then video, then worlds.

  • 2014

    GANs — adversarial generation

    Generator vs. discriminator; the first path to sharp synthetic images.

  • 2018

    StyleGAN — photorealistic faces

    "This person does not exist" makes generative AI's power visible to everyone.

  • 2020

    DDPM — diffusion becomes practical

    Denoising step by step proves more stable and scalable than adversarial training.

  • 2021

    DALL·E — text becomes image

    Open-ended text-to-image generation; CLIP guidance quickly amplifies quality.

  • 2022

    Stable Diffusion, DALL·E 2, Imagen

    Latent diffusion makes generation cheap and open; image generation goes mainstream in a single year.

  • 2023

    ControlNet — controllable generation

    Pose, depth, and edge conditioning turn diffusion from a slot machine into a design tool.

  • 2024

    Sora — video generation at scale

    Minute-long coherent video from text, framed explicitly as a step toward world simulators.

  • 2025–26

    Native audio-video and interactive world models

    Veo-class models add synchronized sound; multiplayer interactive world models make generated environments playable in real time.

Reinforcement Learning

From backgammon to the training signal behind reasoning models.

  • 1992

    TD-Gammon — learning from self-play

    A neural network reaches world-class backgammon through temporal-difference learning alone.

  • 2013

    DQN — pixels to actions

    One network learns dozens of Atari games from raw screens; deep RL is born.

  • 2016

    AlphaGo — the grand-challenge win

    Search plus learned policy and value networks defeat the world's best Go players.

  • 2017

    PPO & AlphaZero

    PPO becomes the field's dependable workhorse; AlphaZero masters Go, chess, and shogi from self-play with no human data.

  • 2020

    MuZero — planning without rules

    Learns a model of the environment's dynamics itself, planning without being told the rules.

  • 2022

    RLHF — RL meets language models

    Human preferences as a reward signal align LLMs; RL becomes central to how frontier models are finished.

  • 2025

    RL for reasoning

    DeepSeek-R1 shows chain-of-thought reasoning can emerge from RL with verifiable rewards — no supervised reasoning data needed.

OpenAI

Set the consumer-AI era in motion and keeps pushing the frontier on reasoning and agents.

  • Nov 2022

    ChatGPT launches

    Reaches 100 million users in two months — the fastest-growing consumer product in history and the starting gun for the current AI race.

  • Mar 2023

    GPT-4

    Multimodal, passes professional exams; becomes the benchmark every rival measures against for nearly two years.

  • 2024

    GPT-4o, o1, and Sora

    Real-time voice and vision (May); o1 introduces the reasoning-model paradigm of thinking before answering (Sep); Sora brings text-to-video to the public (Dec).

  • 2025

    o3, agents, and GPT-5

    Operator and Deep Research turn the models into working agents; GPT-5 (Aug) unifies fast chat and deep reasoning in one system; Codex matures into a production coding agent.

  • 2026

    GPT-5.5 and the GPT-5.6 family

    GPT-5.5 (Apr) targets complex coding and research work; GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna (Jun–Jul) launch publicly after an initial government-requested limited rollout to vetted partners — a first for frontier-model releases.

Anthropic

Safety-focused lab whose models became the default for coding and agentic work.

  • Dec 2022

    Constitutional AI

    Training models to critique themselves against written principles — the alignment method behind every Claude since.

  • 2023

    Claude 1 & 2

    Claude launches with a 100K-token context window, making long-document work practical before anyone else.

  • 2024

    Claude 3, computer use, and MCP

    The Claude 3 family (Mar) is the first to clearly challenge GPT-4; Claude 3.5 Sonnet adds computer use (Oct); the Model Context Protocol (Nov) becomes the industry standard for connecting agents to tools.

  • 2025

    Claude 4 era and Claude Code

    Opus 4 and Sonnet 4 (May) lead coding benchmarks; Claude Code takes agentic coding into terminals everywhere; Sonnet 4.5 and Opus 4.5 (fall) extend the lead in long-horizon agent tasks.

  • 2026

    Opus 4.8 and the Claude 5 family

    Opus 4.8 sharpens the 4-series; the Claude 5 generation debuts with Fable 5 and introduces the Mythos-class tier above Opus — Mythos 5 available to approved organizations.

Google DeepMind

Research powerhouse that fused Brain and DeepMind, spanning LLMs, science, and world models.

  • Dec 2023

    Gemini 1.0

    Google's flagship answer to GPT-4, natively multimodal from the start.

  • 2024

    Long context, AlphaFold 3, and a Nobel Prize

    Gemini 1.5 brings a million-token context (Feb); AlphaFold 3 models all of life's molecules (May); Hassabis and Jumper share the Chemistry Nobel (Oct); Gemini 2.0 and Veo 2 open the agentic push (Dec).

  • 2025

    Gemini 2.5, Veo 3, Genie 3, Gemini 3

    Thinking-native Gemini 2.5 Pro (Mar); Veo 3 generates video with synchronized audio (May); Genie 3 produces playable interactive worlds (Aug); Gemini 3 (Nov) closes the year at the frontier.

  • 2026

    Gemini 3.5 generation

    Gemini 3.5 Flash ships (May); the 3.5 Pro flagship is slated for mid-July with a 2M-token context and a deeper reasoning layer, squaring off against GPT-5.6 and Claude's Fable 5.

Meta AI

The open-weights heavyweight — and lately, the most aggressive talent spender.

  • 2023

    LLaMA and Llama 2

    LLaMA (Feb) escapes the lab and seeds the open-model ecosystem; Llama 2 (Jul) makes open weights commercially usable.

  • 2024

    Llama 3 and the 405B

    Llama 3 (Apr) and 3.1-405B (Jul) deliver the first genuinely frontier-scale open weights.

  • 2025

    Llama 4 and Superintelligence Labs

    Llama 4's MoE multimodal lineup (Apr) lands to mixed reviews; Meta responds by founding Superintelligence Labs and recruiting star researchers with unprecedented offers (Jun).

  • 2026

    Betting on open agentic AI

    Positioning centers on Llama-4-class models as the agentic stack you can run on your own infrastructure — no per-token cost, no vendor lock-in — while the field awaits its next frontier release.

DeepSeek

The efficiency shock: frontier capability at a fraction of the assumed cost.

  • 2024

    V2 and V3

    V2's cheap MoE (May) triggers a price war in China; V3 (Dec) reaches frontier class on a reported ~$5.6M training run.

  • Jan 2025

    R1 — the reasoning shock

    An open reasoning model trained largely by RL matches far more expensive rivals; markets react with the largest single-day value drop in Nvidia's history.

  • 2025

    Iterating under constraints

    V3.1 and V3.2 refine efficiency and agent/tool use while navigating chip-export limits.

  • 2026

    V4 — scaling on domestic silicon

    V4 is reported around April as a ~1T-parameter MoE with a 1M-token context and native multimodality, notably optimized for Huawei Ascend chips rather than Nvidia GPUs.

xAI

  • Nov 2023

    Launches as a real-time, irreverent assistant wired into X.

  • 2024

NVIDIA

The picks-and-shovels winner: nearly every model above was trained on its hardware.

  • 2022

    H100 ships as ChatGPT ignites demand

    The Hopper GPU arrives months before the LLM boom makes it the most sought-after object in tech.

  • 2023

    The trillion-dollar chip company

    Data-center revenue explodes on LLM training demand; NVIDIA joins the $1T club (May) as GPUs become the industry's constraint.

  • 2024

    Blackwell and the top of the market

    Blackwell unveiled at GTC (Mar); NVIDIA briefly becomes the world's most valuable company (Jun) while the CUDA software moat keeps rivals at bay.

  • 2025

    Blackwell ramp, $4T then $5T

    Fastest product ramp in its history; first company past $4T (Jul) and $5T (Oct); releases open Nemotron models and opens NVLink to partners.

  • 2026

    Rubin platform

    Launched at CES (Jan): Vera CPU, Rubin GPU, and NVLink-6 networking promise ~10× lower inference token cost versus Blackwell; cloud deployments begin in the second half of 2026.

  • 2024

  • 2025

    The partnership is restructured (Oct) — Microsoft holds roughly a quarter of OpenAI while gaining independence; first MAI in-house models (MAI-1, MAI-Voice) debut; Fairwater-class AI datacenters come online.

  • 2026

    Vera Rubin early adopter, Maia 200

    Among the first to deploy NVIDIA's Vera Rubin NVL72 racks in next-generation AI datacenters while ramping its own Maia 200 silicon — a two-track compute strategy.

Amazon

  • Jun 2026

Tesla

Embodied AI at scale: neural-network driving, humanoid robots, and in-house silicon.

    AI Days — Dojo and Optimus

    Tesla announces the Dojo training supercomputer and the Optimus humanoid, reframing itself as an AI and robotics company that happens to sell cars.

  • 2024

    End-to-end neural driving, Cybercab unveiled

The Open-Weights Ecosystem

Not one company but a movement — increasingly led from China — that keeps the frontier only months ahead of what anyone can download.

  • 2023

    LLaMA escapes, Mistral arrives

    The LLaMA leak (Feb) and Llama 2's commercial license (Jul) seed the ecosystem; Mistral 7B (Sep) proves a small European team can compete; Hugging Face becomes the movement's home.

  • 2024

    Open weights reach frontier scale

    Alibaba's Qwen 2/2.5 climbs the leaderboards, Llama 3.1-405B delivers the first frontier-scale open weights, and DeepSeek V3 (Dec) matches closed models at a fraction of the training cost.

  • 2025

    The R1 year

    DeepSeek-R1 (Jan) open-sources frontier reasoning and shocks the markets; Qwen 3, Moonshot's 1T-parameter Kimi K2, and Z.ai's GLM-4.5 follow; even OpenAI returns to open weights with gpt-oss (Aug). The closed-to-open gap shrinks to months.

  • 2026

    A release cadence no closed lab matches

    Qwen 3.5/3.6 (Apache 2.0), GLM-5 through 5.2 (MIT), Kimi K2.6 and K2.7-Code, Mistral Medium 3.5, and DeepSeek V4 all ship within months of each other — open models now lead on some agentic-coding benchmarks, with Chinese labs setting the pace.
