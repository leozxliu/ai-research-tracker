Reading the tiers. Big Three marks NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR — the general-ML venues that carry roughly equal weight on a CV. Field flagship marks the leading venue of a subfield: CVPR for vision, ACL for NLP. Top tier venues are strong publications anywhere, and are the primary targets within their own communities. Prestige is subfield-dependent — in vision, CVPR outweighs NeurIPS — and by raw citation impact the order differs again: CVPR has sat at or near the top of Google Scholar's h5-index across all of computer science for years, ahead of NeurIPS. Treat these labels as orientation for newcomers, not a ranking to optimize against.
