Foundations 1950 – 1998

The ideas arrived decades before the hardware could prove them right.

  • 1950

    Computing Machinery and Intelligence

    FoundationsAlan Turing

    Posed "Can machines think?" and proposed the imitation game (Turing test), framing the field before it had a name.

  • 1958

    The Perceptron

    Neural NetsFrank Rosenblatt

    The first trainable artificial neuron — the ancestor of every neural network that followed.

  • 1986

    Learning Representations by Back-Propagating Errors

    Neural NetsRumelhart, Hinton & Williams

    Popularized backpropagation, making multi-layer networks trainable and reviving neural-network research.

  • 1997

    Long Short-Term Memory

    SequencesHochreiter & Schmidhuber

    Solved vanishing gradients for recurrent networks; dominated speech and language modeling for two decades.

  • 1998

    Gradient-Based Learning Applied to Document Recognition (LeNet-5)

    CVLeCun et al.

    Established convolutional neural networks as the way to read images, decades ahead of the hardware.

The Deep-Learning Revolution 2012 – 2016

GPUs plus data turned old ideas into a new field in four years flat.

  • 2012

    ImageNet Classification with Deep CNNs (AlexNet)

    CVKrizhevsky, Sutskever & Hinton

    Crushed the ImageNet benchmark with GPUs and ignited the modern deep-learning era.

  • 2013

    Efficient Estimation of Word Representations (word2vec)

    NLPMikolov et al.

    Showed word meaning could be captured in vectors ("king − man + woman ≈ queen"), starting the embeddings era.

  • 2013

    Playing Atari with Deep Reinforcement Learning (DQN)

    RLMnih et al., DeepMind

    First deep network to learn control from raw pixels, launching deep reinforcement learning.

  • 2014

    Generative Adversarial Networks

    GenerativeGoodfellow et al.

    Two networks competing — generator vs. discriminator — made realistic image generation possible.

  • 2014

    Sequence to Sequence Learning with Neural Networks

    NLPSutskever, Vinyals & Le

    The encoder–decoder recipe behind neural machine translation and, later, chat models.

  • 2014

    Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization

    OptimizationKingma & Ba

    Became the default optimizer for nearly every deep model trained since.

  • 2015

    Deep Residual Learning (ResNet)

    CVHe et al.

    Skip connections let networks go from 20 layers to 1000+, a trick every modern architecture inherits.

  • 2016

    Mastering the Game of Go (AlphaGo)

    RLSilver et al., DeepMind

    Beat the world's best Go players a decade ahead of predictions; deep RL's defining public moment.

Transformers & Scale 2017 – 2021

One architecture, ever-bigger training runs, and the discovery that scale itself is a strategy.

  • 2017

    Attention Is All You Need (Transformer)

    ArchitectureVaswani et al.

    The Transformer replaced recurrence with attention — the architecture underneath every modern LLM.

  • 2018

    BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers

    NLPDevlin et al., Google

    Made "pretrain, then fine-tune" the standard playbook for language understanding.

  • 2020

    Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models

    ScalingKaplan et al., OpenAI

    Showed loss falls predictably with compute, data, and parameters — the argument for building ever-bigger models.

  • 2020

    Language Models are Few-Shot Learners (GPT-3)

    LLMBrown et al., OpenAI

    175B parameters revealed in-context learning: one model, many tasks, no fine-tuning.

  • 2020

    Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models

    GenerativeHo, Jain & Abbeel

    Made diffusion practical, displacing GANs as the engine of image generation.

  • 2020

    An Image is Worth 16x16 Words (ViT)

    CVDosovitskiy et al., Google

    Brought the Transformer to vision, ending CNNs' monopoly on image models.

  • 2021

    Learning Transferable Visual Models (CLIP)

    MultimodalRadford et al., OpenAI

    Tied images and text into one embedding space — the connective tissue of multimodal AI.

  • 2021

    Highly Accurate Protein Structure Prediction (AlphaFold 2)

    AI for ScienceJumper et al., DeepMind

    Effectively solved protein folding, a 50-year grand challenge; later earned a Nobel Prize in Chemistry.

The LLM Era 2022 – today

Models become assistants, weights go open, and reasoning becomes trainable.

  • 2022

    Training LMs to Follow Instructions (InstructGPT)

    AlignmentOuyang et al., OpenAI

    RLHF turned raw language models into helpful assistants — the technique behind ChatGPT.

  • 2022

    Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning

    ReasoningWei et al., Google

    "Think step by step" unlocked latent reasoning in LLMs and seeded today's reasoning models.

  • 2022

    Training Compute-Optimal LLMs (Chinchilla)

    ScalingHoffmann et al., DeepMind

    Showed most LLMs were undertrained on data, resetting how the field balances model size vs. tokens.

  • 2022

    High-Resolution Image Synthesis with Latent Diffusion

    GenerativeRombach et al.

    Ran diffusion in latent space, making it cheap — the paper behind Stable Diffusion.

  • 2023

    LLaMA: Open and Efficient Foundation Language Models

    Open ModelsTouvron et al., Meta

    Put near-frontier weights in researchers' hands and ignited the open-model ecosystem.

  • 2023

    Mamba: Linear-Time Sequence Modeling

    ArchitectureGu & Dao

    State-space models as the first credible post-Transformer challenger for long sequences.

  • 2025

    DeepSeek-R1: Incentivizing Reasoning via RL

    ReasoningDeepSeek

    Showed reasoning ability can emerge from pure reinforcement learning — and released it openly at a fraction of frontier cost.
